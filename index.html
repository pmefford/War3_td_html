Bastion TD

Build a maze. Upgrade your towers. Survive 40 waves.

  • Place towers on the grid to build a maze. Enemies walk spawn → ① → ② → exit; you can lengthen their trip but never fully block it.
  • Tap a tower to upgrade it (3 levels), then pick one of two specialisations. Selling returns 70%.
  • Armored foes (🛡️) shrug off physical hits – use poison, frost, storm. Warded (🔮) resist magic – use cannons & arrows. Flyers (🐉) ignore the maze – you need anti-air.
  • Send waves early for bonus gold. Every 10th wave is a boss; bosses cost 5 lives if they leak.
  • Desktop keys: 1-6 pick tower, Space send wave, U upgrade, S sell, P pause, Esc cancel.
